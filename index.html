Homemaking and Companion Services

How we can help

Team members provide non-medical, hands-off support to help older adults, individuals with disabilities, or recovering patients live independently at home. These services generally focus on maintaining the household, running errands, and offering social interaction rather than hands-on personal or nursing care.

Now Serving: Brevard, Orange, Osceola, and Seminole Counties.

Core Homemaker Services

These services focus on household management and daily routines to keep the living space clean, safe, and organized:

  • ✓
    Light Housekeeping: Dusting, vacuuming, washing dishes, and taking out the trash.
  • ✓
    Laundry Services: Washing, drying, folding, and ironing clothes.
  • ✓
    Meal Preparation: Planning menus, preparing meals, and cleaning up afterward.
  • ✓
    Grocery Shopping: Buying food and household supplies based on dietary needs.

Companion Services

Companion care focuses on social interaction and emotional well-being to prevent isolation and ensure safety:

  • ✓
    Socialization: Engaging in friendly conversation, playing board games, or sharing hobbies.
  • ✓
    Supervision and Safety: Keeping an eye on the individual to provide a safe environment and companionship while family caregivers are away.
  • ✓
    Routine Assistance: Providing gentle reminders for daily tasks, hydration, and appointments.

Errand and Support Services

Agencies also assist with getting out of the house and managing life outside the home:

  • ✓
    Transportation: Driving clients or arranging rides to medical appointments, social events, or community outings.
  • ✓
    Running Errands: Picking up prescriptions from the pharmacy, dry cleaning, or going to the post office.

Career Opportunities

Join Our Care Team

Festar Health is seeking compassionate, dependable professionals to provide non-medical homemaking and companion services throughout Brevard, Orange, Osceola and Seminole Counties.

View Qualifications & Screening Requirements
Applicants must:
  • Be legally authorized to work in the United States
  • Successfully complete an AHCA Level 2 fingerprint-based background screening
  • Hold a valid driver’s license, maintain current automobile insurance and have reliable transportation
  • Demonstrate a safe driving history when transporting clients
  • Communicate clearly and treat clients with dignity, patience and respect
  • Be dependable, professional and able to perform assigned household and companion duties
  • Understand that homemaker and companion positions do not include hands-on personal care, nursing or therapy
Bilingual applicants are strongly encouraged to apply. Relevant caregiving, homemaking or companion experience is preferred.
AHCA requires Level 2 screening before new homemakers and companions are hired. “U.S. citizenship” should not be listed as a requirement; the appropriate qualification is legal authorization to work in the United States. Driving and bilingual ability are Festar employment qualifications rather than general AHCA mandates. View AHCA hiring guidance and AHCA background-screening guidance.
