Career Opportunities

Join Our Care Team

Festar Health is seeking compassionate, dependable professionals to provide non-medical homemaking and companion services throughout Brevard, Orange, Osceola and Seminole Counties.

Now serving Brevard, Orange, Osceola and Seminole Counties.

Applicant Qualifications

Applicants must:
  • ✓
    Be legally authorized to work in the United States
  • ✓
    Successfully complete an AHCA Level 2 fingerprint-based background screening
  • ✓
    Hold a valid driver’s license, maintain current automobile insurance and have reliable transportation
  • ✓
    Demonstrate a safe driving history when transporting clients
  • ✓
    Communicate clearly and treat clients with dignity, patience and respect
  • ✓
    Be dependable, professional and able to perform assigned household and companion duties
  • ✓
    Understand that homemaker and companion positions do not include hands-on personal care, nursing or therapy
Bilingual applicants are strongly encouraged to apply. Relevant caregiving, homemaking or companion experience is preferred.

AHCA Screening & Hiring Information

AHCA requires Level 2 screening before new homemakers and companions are hired. “U.S. citizenship” is not a requirement; the appropriate qualification is legal authorization to work in the United States. Driving and bilingual ability are Festar employment qualifications rather than general AHCA mandates.
