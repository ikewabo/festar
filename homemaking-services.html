Homemaking and Companion Services

Practical help. Meaningful companionship. Greater independence at home.

Festar Heritage provides non-medical, hands-off assistance for older adults, individuals with disabilities, and people recovering at home. Our team helps maintain a comfortable household, supports everyday routines, runs errands, and provides dependable companionship.

(321) 278-4165
Now serving Brevard, Orange, Osceola and Seminole Counties.

Important Service Boundary

Festar’s homemakers and companions do not provide bathing, dressing, toileting, feeding, lifting, transfers, medication administration, wound care, nursing, therapy or other hands-on personal care.
