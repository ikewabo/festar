Therapy Appointment Coordination

Helping clients and families stay organized around prescribed therapy.

When a physician or licensed provider recommends physical, occupational or speech therapy, Festar can help clients and families manage the practical details surrounding those services.

(321) 278-4165
Now serving Brevard, Orange, Osceola and Seminole Counties.

Coordination support may include

  • ✓
    Identifying appropriate licensed therapy providers
  • ✓
    Helping families contact therapy offices
  • ✓
    Scheduling and confirming appointments
  • ✓
    Coordinating transportation or appointment accompaniment
  • ✓
    Maintaining calendars and providing appointment reminders
  • ✓
    Helping organize non-clinical paperwork
  • ✓
    Communicating scheduling information to authorized family members
  • ✓
    Encouraging clients to follow instructions provided by their licensed therapist
Important Non-Clinical Coordination Notice (Florida Statute 400.509) Festar Heritage LLC is a registered Homemaker and Companion Services Provider (Florida AHCA Registration #242148, File #39980634). Our therapy coordination services are strictly limited to non-clinical appointment scheduling, calendar reminders, paperwork organization, and ride accompaniment. Festar Heritage does not provide physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, nursing, diagnosis, medical treatment, or clinical patient management. All clinical therapy services must be ordered, delivered, and supervised by independent, appropriately licensed healthcare professionals.
